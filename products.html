Breads

Our breads are baked fresh daily. You can select from our wide range of breads and the size that fits best. We offer full loaves and half loaves. Ask us about sampling bread with your choice of melted butter, garlic butter, or add cinnamon for a different twist.

    Pastries

    We bake a wide range of pastries daily. Flaky croissants to warm and soft cinnamon rolls. Seasonal offerings are dependent upon availability. Stop in during your morning commute to pick up a fresh start to your day.

      Cakes

      Our cakes are made from scratch with real butter and fresh cream, perfect for birthdays, weddings, and community events. Custom orders can be placed through our pre-order form with at least one week's notice.
